## indentation
PEP 8 recommends that you use four spaces per indentation level. 
Python interpreter gets confused when tabs are mixed with spaces. 


## line length

- comments to 72 characters per line, 

## blank lines
You should use blank lines to organize your files, but don’t do so excessively. 
- Blank lines won’t affect how your code runs, but they will affect the readability of your code.
