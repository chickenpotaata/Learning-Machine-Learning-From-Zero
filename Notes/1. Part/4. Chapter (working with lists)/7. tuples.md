A tuple looks just like a list except you use parentheses instead of square
brackets.
- you can access its individual elements just like on a list 
- you cannot mutate the elements inside a tuple after its creation



## Looping Through All Values in a Tuple

```python
dimensions = (200, 50)
for dimension in dimensions:
	print(dimension)

```
